Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want you to know exactly where you stand before you pay. This policy applies to all Fyzzo Voyage services.

1. Our fees are non-refundable

As soon as you pay, we start work on your file and commit time and costs on your behalf. For this reason, all fees paid to Fyzzo Voyage are non-refundable, including if you change your mind, withdraw, or stop responding. Please ask every question you have before you pay.

2. How we stand behind your Study Abroad application

If this happensWhat we do
Your visa is refused by UKVIWe review the refusal with you and prepare your re-application at no extra Fyzzo Voyage fee, if you reapply within 6 months. You pay the government fees again.
Your CAS is refused, or the intake becomes too lateWe move your file to the next available intake at no extra cost, if you confirm within 30 days that you want to continue. After 30 days, a ₦150,000 re-activation fee applies.
An application fails because of a clear error by Fyzzo VoyageWe redo the work at no cost to you.

3. Other services

  • Visit, family and business visa support: non-refundable once paid. If your visa is refused, we review the refusal with you free of charge.
  • Refusal reviews, proof of funds reviews, document reviews and interview coaching: non-refundable once paid. Sessions can be rescheduled once with 24 hours' notice.
  • Flights and holidays: our search or booking fee is non-refundable. Ticket and hotel refunds follow the airline's or hotel's own rules.

4. Payments to third parties

Visa fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge, university fees, TB tests, flights and hotels are paid directly to those providers, and their own refund rules apply. We will help you request a refund from them where one is possible.

5. Special circumstances

In cases of serious illness, bereavement or events outside anyone's control, management may agree to move your file to a later intake. These are decided case by case with supporting documents. Email fyzzovoyage@gmail.com with your name, agreement reference and details.

Visa decisions are made solely by the relevant embassy, immigration authority or institution. Fyzzo Voyage does not guarantee visa approval, admission or immigration outcomes, and does not create, alter or provide financial or other documents.
