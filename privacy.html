Privacy & Data Protection Policy

How Fyzzo Voyage handles your personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who we are

The data controller is Fyzzo Global Ltd, trading as Fyzzo Voyage, Lagos, Nigeria. For privacy questions, email fyzzovoyage@gmail.com or call +234 806 249 9796.

2. What we collect

  • When you contact us (website forms, eligibility quiz, advert lead forms, WhatsApp, calls): your name, phone/WhatsApp, email, country, destination, the service you need, your timeline and budget, and what you tell us about your situation.
  • During a paid service: passport and ID details, academic records, CV, references, financial and sponsor documents, travel history, previous refusals, photos and anything else your application needs.
  • When you use the website: device and usage data, only through the tools described in section 7.

3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis (NDPA s.25)
Replying to your enquiry and assessing eligibilityYour consent, and steps you ask for before a contract
Providing the service you paid forPerformance of our agreement with you
Keeping accounting and tax recordsLegal obligation
Sending updates about intakes and offersYour consent (you can opt out any time)
Measuring and improving our adverts and websiteYour consent to cookies

4. Who we share it with

  • Universities and colleges you choose to apply to.
  • Embassies, immigration authorities and their visa application centres, when you apply.
  • Our authorised service providers, who process data for us under written confidentiality and data-protection terms.
  • Technology providers we use to run the business: Google (email, Drive, forms), Meta (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and advertising tools) and our website form service, FormSubmit.

We never sell your data or share it with others for their own marketing.

5. International transfers

To apply abroad, your data must be sent to universities and authorities in the destination country (for example the United Kingdom), and it is stored with cloud providers that may be outside Nigeria. We only transfer data where the NDPA allows it, for example where it is necessary for the service you asked for, or with appropriate safeguards in place.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become clients are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for 6 years after the service ends, for legal and accounting reasons, then securely deleted.

7. Cookies and tracking

Today this website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It only remembers, for your current visit, whether you have already seen the free-guide pop-up. If we add tools such as the Meta Pixel or Google Analytics, they will only switch on after you accept them in a cookie notice, and you will be able to change your choice at any time.

8. How we protect it

Access is limited to staff who need it, accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step verification, and documents are stored in access-controlled folders. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Email fyzzovoyage@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

10. Children

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to our service and sign on your behalf.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the new effective date here. Significant changes will be shared with current clients.

Visa decisions are made solely by the relevant embassy, immigration authority or institution. Fyzzo Voyage does not guarantee visa approval, admission or immigration outcomes, and does not create, alter or provide financial or other documents.
